Bintu Fatimoh Ajanaku, who holds a 33% stake in the company, is a visionary leader whose influence is particularly strong in the oil and gas industry. With deep expertise in global energy markets, she played a crucial role in the company’s successful entry into this competitive field. Bintu’s strategic thinking has been key in forming important partnerships and advocating for sustainability, helping the company establish itself as a leader in the sector. She champions innovation, particularly through supporting advanced technologies that improve operational efficiency in drilling and exploration. Additionally, Bintu is committed to incorporating renewable energy into traditional oil and gas operations, ensuring the company thrives now and remains sustainable in the future. A dedicated advocate for women in the energy industry, she actively works to create leadership opportunities for women in the traditionally male- dominated sector. Bintu’s global influence extends to negotiating exploration agreements with multinational corporations, unlocking valuable oil reserves across the world. As a director, she continues to guide the company’s success, using her expertise to navigate market challenges. Her forward- thinking approach to integrating clean energy and reducing fossil fuel dependence positions the company for long-term profitability and sustainability. Bintu earned a BSc in Public Administration from the University of Lagos.
