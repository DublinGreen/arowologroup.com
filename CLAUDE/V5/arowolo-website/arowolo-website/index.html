About Arowolo Group

Arowolo Group of Companies Limited, incorporated in 2010, is an indigenous and integrated service provider within the energy industry. We offer solutions across the energy value chain, focusing on Engineering, Procurement, Construction and Installation (EPCI), gas solutions (CNG), and the importation, retail and bulk distribution of petroleum products.

With a vast network of Original Equipment Manufacturers (OEMs), our technical expertise and a highly skilled workforce, we deliver bespoke solutions to customers and stakeholders. Our operations are driven by well-structured processes, global best practices and a customer-centric approach.

Beyond our core business, we support host communities through our charitable foundation, the Arowolo Royal Foundation (ARF), with sustained interventions and infrastructure projects.
