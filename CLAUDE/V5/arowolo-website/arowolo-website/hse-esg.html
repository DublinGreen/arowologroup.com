HSE & ESG

Health, safety and environment

Through our commitment to safety, health and environmental excellence, we affirm to all our stakeholders, including our employees, customers, shareholders and the public, that we will work collaboratively to bring sustainable, market-driven and innovative solutions to some of the world’s biggest challenges, making lives better, safer and healthier for people everywhere.

We will implement strategies that build successful businesses and achieve the greatest benefit for all our stakeholders, with respect and care for the environment and without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their needs.

We will continuously improve our practices in light of advances in technology and new understanding in safety, health and environmental science. We will make consistent, measurable progress in implementing this commitment throughout our nationwide operations, and support Responsible Care as a key programme for achieving it.

Our HSE commitments

  • Highest standards of performance
  • Business excellence
  • Zero injuries, illnesses and incidents
  • Zero waste and emissions
  • Conservation of natural resources, energy and biodiversity
  • Continuously improving processes, practices and products
  • Open and public discussion
  • Influence on public policy
  • Management and employee commitment
  • Accountability

ESG policy

Our environmental, social and governance commitments shape how we run every one of our businesses.

Environmental

We support the Sustainable Development Goal on climate action by reducing our environmental footprint through resource efficiency, waste reduction and energy conservation.

Social

Across our portfolio we uphold fair labour practices, human rights and a diverse, inclusive workplace, and support local communities through social impact and development programmes.

Governance

We maintain high standards of business ethics, including transparency, integrity and accountability, in all our business sectors.

Board oversight

Our board oversees ESG strategy, compliance, performance and risk. A sustainability report aligned to Global Reporting Standards is disclosed quarterly in the board pack.

Protecting the environment in practice

Our waste management and remediation teams respond to spills, clean shorelines and restore affected sites.
